(2022), *Interpretability in the Wild* (arXiv:2211.00593). the six it missed reach the logits only by way of another head, which is precisely what total-effect patching cant resolve. this phase measures single paths instead.

## 1. what path patching does differently
Activation patching splices a node's clean value into the corrupted run and lets the change propagate everywhere. path patching pins every *other* attention head to its corrupted value first, so the only thing that can reach the chosen receiver is what came straight from the sender. Following Wang et al., MLPs are recomputed rather than pinned: the paper treats attention heads as the circuit's nodes and lets an MLP carry a path between them.

A head's q, k and v at token position *p* are computed from the residual stream at *p* alone, so only writes at *p* can reach them. Sender and receiver positions are therefore always equal, not a simplification, an architectural constraint.

## 2. the search, round by round
Round 0 asks which heads reach the logits directly. each later round takes the heads found in the round before, the top 4 by absolute effect, a width fixed in advance, and asks which heads feed them. the answer key is never used to choose receivers, so a wrong turn in one round propagates rather than being silently corrected.

what the *receivers* are is prior knowledge: that S-inhibition heads act on name movers' queries, and so on, comes from the paper's account of the mechanism. which *senders* turn up isnt constrained, all 144 heads are swept every round. this is guided rediscovery, not blind search, and phase 3 would have to search receiver inputs too.

### the dedicated probe

Taking each half from where it is sound: the receivers are the heads the `s2_swap` chain discovered at round 2, arrived at without consulting the answer key, and the measurement runs on `abc`, the only scheme in which S1+1 differs at all. mixing schemes is a real caveat, the receivers were identified under one counterfactual and probed under another, and it is the price of the question being answerable.

A sweep can only test senders below its earliest receiver, so the probe is run once per distinct receiver layer. dropping the earliest receivers raises the ceiling and brings deeper senders into range. Running every variant is what stops the sender range from quietly excluding a head.

the two measurements disagree, and the disagreement is the finding. `2.2`, `4.11` each deliver a substantial share of the receiver's clean-vs-corrupted difference, the path is there and carries signal, while their effect on the output logit difference stays near zero.

The two columns answer different questions, which is why both are reported. *Path effect on logits* asks whether the prediction moves. *signal at receiver* asks whether the path delivered anything at its own endpoint. A path can score full marks on the second and near zero on the first, bcuz every stage downstream of the receiver is still running on corrupted input, the deeper a link sits in the chain, the more of its effect is absorbed before reaching the output.

Where that pattern holds, the defensible conclusion is that logit-difference path patching is the wrong instrument for that link, not that the link is absent. making it measurable needs a metric defined at the receiver rather than at the output. that is a change of measurement, not of method, and it is left for a later phase rather than folded into this one's headline number, the previous-token heads are counted as misses in every table above.

## 5. what this phase settled
Path patching on its own recovers 19/26 published heads. combined with phase 1's activation patching the project recovers **20/26**, unchanged from phase 1's 20/26.

No head missed by phase 1 was recovered here. That is a negative result and is reported as one: on this task, with this metric, path patching didnt reach past what total-effect patching already found.

still outstanding, duplicate token: 0.10. previous token: 2.2, 4.11. induction: 5.8. backup name mover: 9.0, 11.9. section 4 covers the previous-token case.

### what did improve

Head count isnt the only thing a circuit claim is made of, and two things moved that the 20/26 headline doesnt show.

precision. phase 1 discovered 28 heads to find 20, a precision of 0.71. phase 2 discovered 21 to find 19, a precision of 0.90. restricting each measurement to a single path removes most of what total-effect patching swept up incidentally.

the mechanism, not just the parts. phase 1 produced a ranked list of heads. the chain here reproduced the paper's causal *order* without being told it, each round's receivers coming from the round before: round 0-4/4 top senders in the published circuit (name mover, negative name mover). Round 1-4/4 top senders in the published circuit (s-inhibition). Round 2-4/4 top senders in the published circuit (duplicate token, induction). Round 3, no signal (every sender exactly zero). recovering the wiring is a stronger claim than recovering the set of components, and it is the claim phase 1 couldnt make at all.

The direct-effect round is worth reading on its own. heads that phase 1 ranked highly by total effect can score near zero on direct effect, which isnt a contradiction: it is the measurement saying their influence is real but routed through another head. that distinction is exactly what phase 1 couldnt draw, and drawing it is what this phase was for.

One deliberate choice worth stating plainly. every head counted as discovered above was discovered by its effect on the output logit difference, the same criterion phase 1 used, kept identical so the two phases are comparable. the *signal at receiver* column repeatedly identifies published heads that the logit criterion misses: the previous-token heads in section 4 are the clearest case, and the S-inhibition heads under `abc` are another. scoring discovery on that column instead would raise the recall number in this report.

it wasnt done, bcuz switching the success criterion after seeing which criterion scores better is how a validation exercise stops validating anything. the signal metric is reported as a diagnostic, its disagreements with the logit metric are shown wherever they occur, and adopting it as a discovery criterion is left to a later phase where it can be committed to in advance.
